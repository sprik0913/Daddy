# Prayer room. Same format. "prompts:" are short guided lines (|| separated).

## acts | ACTS: A simple pattern
refs: PSA.100.1-5 ; 1JN.1.9 ; PHP.4.6
summary: Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving, Supplication.
prompts: Adoration: Praise God for who he is. Name one of his attributes. || Confession: Tell him honestly where you sinned. || Thanksgiving: Thank him for three specific things from today. || Supplication: Ask for what you and others need.

## lords | Praying the Lord’s Prayer
refs: MAT.6.9-13
summary: Jesus’ own model, line by line.
prompts: Our Father in heaven: Thank him that you are his son. || Hallowed be your name: Praise him. || Your kingdom come, your will be done: Surrender your plans. || Give us today our daily bread: Ask for what you need today. || Forgive us as we forgive: Confess, and release someone who hurt you. || Lead us not into temptation: Ask for strength where you are weak.

## wife | For your wife
refs: EPH.5.25-28 ; 1PE.3.7
summary: Pray for her heart, her health, her walk with God and your marriage.
prompts: Thank God for one specific quality in her. || Pray for her burdens today. || Ask God to make you a better husband to her. || Pray for unity and joy in your marriage.

## children | For your children
refs: PSA.127.3-5 ; 3JN.1.4
summary: Pray for their faith, character, friends and future.
prompts: Pray each child’s name and their current need. || Ask God to draw them to himself. || Pray for their friendships and protection. || Ask for patience and wisdom as their father.

## work | For your work
refs: COL.3.23-24 ; PRO.16.3
summary: Commit your work to the Lord.
prompts: Offer today’s work to God. || Pray for coworkers by name. || Ask for integrity in hard decisions. || Ask God for opportunities to serve.

## temptation | In temptation
refs: 1CO.10.13 ; HEB.4.15-16 ; JAS.4.7
summary: Draw near to the throne of grace.
prompts: Tell God honestly what is tempting you. || Thank him that Jesus was tempted and did not sin. || Ask for the way of escape he promises. || Text a brother now.

## anxiety | When anxious
refs: PHP.4.6-7 ; 1PE.5.7 ; PSA.56.3-4
summary: Cast your anxiety on him because he cares for you.
prompts: Name what you are anxious about. || Hand each worry to God one at a time. || Thank him for something he has already done. || Ask for his peace that passes understanding.

## grief | In grief
refs: PSA.34.18 ; JHN.11.35 ; 2CO.1.3-4
summary: The Lord is near the brokenhearted.
prompts: Tell God exactly how you feel. || Remember that Jesus wept. || Ask him to comfort you. || Ask him to bring people around you.

## brothers | For your brothers
refs: EPH.6.18 ; JAS.5.16
summary: Pray for the men in your crew.
prompts: Pray for each brother by name. || Pray for their marriages and families. || Pray for their battles. || Pray that they would finish strong.

## lost | For those who don’t know Christ
refs: ROM.10.1 ; COL.4.2-6 ; 1TI.2.1-4
summary: Pray for people by name and for open doors.
prompts: Name three people who don’t know Jesus. || Ask God to open their hearts. || Ask for an opportunity to talk with them. || Ask for courage and gentleness.

## nation | For leaders and nation
refs: 1TI.2.1-2 ; JER.29.7
summary: Pray for those in authority and for the peace of your city.
prompts: Pray for national and local leaders. || Pray for your pastor and church. || Pray for justice and peace in your community. || Pray for revival.

## personal | A personal prayer
refs: PSA.139.23-24 ; HEB.4.16
summary: Come boldly to the throne of grace with whatever is on your heart.
prompts: Be still for a moment and remember God is with you. || Tell him what is on your mind, plainly. || Ask him to search your heart. || Write down one thing you sense he is saying through Scripture.

## finances | For financial wisdom
refs: PRO.3.9-10 ; PHP.4.19 ; MAT.6.31-33 ; PRO.22.7
summary: Seek first his kingdom. Ask for wisdom, contentment and generosity.
prompts: Thank God for his provision. || Confess any fear, greed or debt that weighs on you. || Ask for wisdom for a specific money decision. || Ask for a generous heart.

## hardship | In hardship
refs: PSA.46.1-3 ; 2CO.4.8-9 ; ROM.8.28 ; ISA.43.2
summary: God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.
prompts: Tell God honestly what is hard right now. || Remember one time he was faithful before. || Ask for strength for today, not all of tomorrow. || Ask who you can lean on.

## growth | For spiritual growth
refs: COL.1.9-12 ; EPH.3.14-19 ; 2PE.3.18
summary: Pray Paul’s prayers for yourself and others.
prompts: Ask to be filled with knowledge of his will. || Ask to grasp how wide and deep Christ’s love is. || Ask for fruit in one specific area. || Thank him for growth you have seen.

## marriage | For your marriage
refs: ECC.4.12 ; COL.3.12-14 ; 1CO.13.4-7
summary: A cord of three strands is not quickly broken.
prompts: Thank God for your wife and your marriage. || Confess where you have been impatient or unkind. || Pray 1 Corinthians 13 over your marriage. || Ask for unity, joy and faithfulness.
