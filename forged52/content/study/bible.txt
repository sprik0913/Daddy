# Understand the Bible. Sections with items: "item: Title | label | text | refs"
# Dates are approximate and follow commonly used conservative scholarship; some are debated.

## timeline | The Story of the Bible
item: Creation and fall | Beginning | God creates a good world and humanity in his image. Adam and Eve rebel, and sin and death enter. God promises a descendant who will crush the serpent. | GEN.1.1 ; GEN.3.15
item: The flood and Babel | Early history | Humanity grows violent. God judges with the flood, saves Noah and covenants with creation. Babel shows human pride scattered. | GEN.6.5-8 ; GEN.9.8-17
item: The patriarchs | c. 2000–1800 BC | God calls Abraham and promises land, offspring and blessing for all nations. The promise passes to Isaac, Jacob and Joseph. | GEN.12.1-3
item: Exodus and Sinai | c. 1446 BC (some date c. 1260 BC) | God rescues Israel from Egypt through Moses, gives the law at Sinai and dwells among them in the tabernacle. | EXO.19.4-6 ; EXO.20.1-3
item: Conquest and judges | c. 1400–1050 BC | Joshua leads Israel into Canaan. Israel cycles through sin, oppression, crying out and rescue under the judges. | JOS.21.43-45 ; JDG.21.25
item: The kingdom | c. 1050–930 BC | Saul, David and Solomon reign. God promises David an eternal throne. Solomon builds the temple. | 2SA.7.12-16
item: Divided kingdom | 930–586 BC | Israel splits into north (Israel) and south (Judah). Prophets call both to return. Assyria conquers the north (722 BC). | 1KI.12.16-20
item: Exile | 586–538 BC | Babylon destroys Jerusalem and the temple. Daniel and Ezekiel minister in exile. God promises a new covenant. | 2KI.25.8-11 ; JER.31.31-34
item: Return | 538–430 BC | Persia allows the Jews to return. The temple and walls are rebuilt under Zerubbabel, Ezra and Nehemiah. | EZR.1.1-4
item: Between the testaments | 430–5 BC | Around 400 years of no new canonical prophecy. Greek then Roman rule. Synagogues and expectation for the Messiah grow. | MAL.4.5-6
item: Jesus | c. 5 BC–AD 30/33 | The Son of God becomes man, proclaims the kingdom, dies for sin and rises from the dead. | JHN.1.14 ; 1CO.15.3-4
item: The church | AD 30–95 | The Spirit comes at Pentecost. The gospel spreads from Jerusalem to Rome. The apostles write the New Testament. | ACT.1.8
item: New creation | Future | Christ returns, judges the world and makes all things new. God dwells with his people forever. | REV.21.1-5

## covenants | The Covenants
item: Noah | Genesis 9 | God promises never again to destroy the earth by flood. Sign: the rainbow. | GEN.9.8-17
item: Abraham | Genesis 12, 15, 17 | God promises land, descendants and blessing to all nations through Abraham’s offspring. Sign: circumcision. | GEN.15.4-6
item: Moses (Sinai) | Exodus 19–24 | God makes Israel his treasured people and gives his law. Sign: the Sabbath. | EXO.19.5-6
item: David | 2 Samuel 7 | God promises David a son whose throne will last forever. | 2SA.7.16
item: The new covenant | Jeremiah 31; Luke 22 | God promises to write his law on hearts and forgive sin forever. Jesus inaugurates it with his blood. | JER.31.33-34 ; LUK.22.20

## genres | How to Read Each Kind of Book
item: Law | Genesis–Deuteronomy | Story and instruction. Ask what it shows about God’s holiness and how Christ fulfills it. | MAT.5.17
item: History | Joshua–Esther, Acts | True stories of God’s faithfulness. Not every action described is approved. | ROM.15.4
item: Poetry and wisdom | Job–Song of Songs | Feel, pray and ponder. Proverbs are general principles, not promises. | PSA.1.1-3
item: Prophets | Isaiah–Malachi | God’s messengers calling people back to covenant, with judgment and hope. | 2PE.1.21
item: Gospels | Matthew–John | Four eyewitness-based portraits of Jesus. | JHN.20.31
item: Letters | Romans–Jude | Written to real churches. Read the whole letter and its situation. | 2TI.3.16
item: Apocalyptic | Daniel, Revelation | Symbolic visions. Main point: God wins and Christ reigns. | REV.1.1-3
