M|1|Tell your family what you're doing this year and why. Ask them to hold you to it.
M|2|Pray out loud with your wife, or a close brother if you're single. Short is fine.
M|3|At dinner, ask each person: "What's one thing you're thankful for today?"
M|4|Call your dad, or a man who's been like a father to you, and thank him for one specific thing.
M|5|Do one chore at home you normally leave to someone else, without being asked or mentioning it.
M|6|Pray over each of your kids by name before bed. If you don't have kids, pray for a family in your church by name.
M|7|Write your wife a note telling her one thing you respect about her. Hide it where she'll find it.
M|8|Turn the phone off at dinner every night this week. All of them.
M|9|Ask someone you've wronged to forgive you. Don't explain it away. Just own it.
M|10|Take one of your kids, or a young man in your life, out for breakfast. Ask about their life and listen.
M|11|Read one of this week's chapters out loud to your family.
M|12|Give something away that costs you. Money, time or a skill.
M|13|Ask your wife: "How can I pray for you this week?" Then actually pray for it every day.
M|14|Tell your kids a story about a time God came through for you.
M|15|Fast from something you lean on (a meal, social media, sports talk) for one day and pray in that time.
M|16|Encourage another man by text. Tell him specifically what you see God doing in him.
M|17|Get up 15 minutes before everyone else every day this week and spend it with God before your phone.
M|18|Bless your kids out loud before school or bed: "I'm proud of you. God has a plan for you."
M|19|Plan a date with your wife. You make every arrangement.
M|20|Ask your family: "Is there anything I do that makes it hard to talk to me?" Listen without defending yourself.
M|21|Serve someone outside your family this week: a neighbor, a widow, a single mom.
M|22|Memorize this week's verse together as a family.
M|23|Write down three ways God has provided for your family this year and share them at dinner.
M|24|Apologize to one of your kids for a time you got angry. Show them what humility looks like.
M|25|Invite a man who's alone to dinner or to watch the game with you.
M|26|Halfway. Tell your family one thing God has changed in you since you started.
M|27|Pray with your wife about one decision you're facing together.
M|28|Teach one of your kids or a younger man a skill you know. Use the time to talk about faith.
M|29|Go one full day without complaining. If you slip, start over.
M|30|Ask an older believer you respect for advice on one area of your life.
M|31|Write down your family's "why": what you want your home to stand for. Talk about it together.
M|32|Pay for someone's meal or groceries quietly.
M|33|Ask your kids what they think about God. Don't correct. Just listen and learn.
M|34|Take a prayer walk around your neighborhood and pray for each house you pass.
M|35|Thank your pastor or a church leader in person.
M|36|Read Ephesians 5:25-33 and ask your wife how you can love her better.
M|37|Have a screen-free evening with your family. Play a game, cook or go outside.
M|38|Pray for a man you don't get along with, every day this week.
M|39|Three-quarters done. Recommit out loud to your family to finish strong.
M|40|Share your faith story with someone who doesn't follow Jesus. Keep it simple and honest.
M|41|Ask your kids to pray for you, and tell them what to pray for.
M|42|Clean up something you've been putting off at home: a project, a room, a repair.
M|43|Send a handwritten thank-you to someone who has invested in your family.
M|44|Take your wife's or a friend's burden for a day. Find out what's weighing on them and carry part of it.
M|45|Choose a family verse and write it somewhere everyone sees it every day.
M|46|Talk with your family about how you spend money and what generosity looks like for you.
M|47|Check in on a man who has drifted from church or from God. Just ask how he's really doing.
M|48|Tell each person in your family one thing you love about them.
M|49|Plan how you'll keep reading after the 52 weeks are done.
M|50|Pray for your future grandkids and the generations after you.
M|51|Write a letter to your kids about what you learned this year. Keep it for them.
M|52|Finish. Celebrate with your family, and call out the next man to take the challenge.
