B|ROM|Paul|Written from Corinth around AD 57 to the church in Rome|The gospel explained: everyone has sinned, and God makes sinners right with him by grace through faith in Jesus, then transforms how they live.|Jesus is the center: "There is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus" (8:1).
B|1CO|Paul|Written from Ephesus around AD 55 to a messy church in Corinth|Paul tackles division, sexual sin, lawsuits, worship and spiritual gifts, and points to love and the resurrection.|"Christ crucified" (1:23) and Christ risen (15) answer every problem.
B|2CO|Paul|Around AD 56, after a painful conflict with Corinth|Paul defends his ministry and shows that God's power shows up in weakness.|"God made him who had no sin to be sin for us, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God" (5:21).
B|GAL|Paul|Around AD 48-55, to churches in Galatia|Don't add law-keeping to the gospel; we are justified by faith in Christ and set free to walk by the Spirit.|"I have been crucified with Christ… Christ lives in me" (2:20).
B|EPH|Paul, from prison|Around AD 60-62|Everything God has given us in Christ (chapters 1-3), and how to live it out (4-6): unity, new life, family and spiritual battle.|God's plan is to unite all things in Christ (1:10).
B|PHP|Paul, from prison|Around AD 61|A thank-you letter full of joy in hard circumstances: "to live is Christ and to die is gain."|The hymn of 2:5-11: Jesus humbled himself to the cross and is exalted above every name.
B|COL|Paul, from prison|Around AD 60-62|Jesus is supreme over everything, so you don't need anything added to him.|"He is the image of the invisible God… in him all things hold together" (1:15-17).
B|1TH|Paul|From Corinth around AD 50-51, one of his earliest letters|Encouragement to a young persecuted church: keep growing, live holy, and have hope in Jesus' return.|The Lord himself will come down from heaven (4:16).
B|2TH|Paul|A few months after 1 Thessalonians|The Day of the Lord hasn't come yet; stand firm and keep working.|Jesus will return and overcome evil.
B|1TI|Paul|Around AD 62-64, to his young co-worker Timothy in Ephesus|How to lead a church: guard sound teaching, pray, appoint qualified leaders and fight the good fight.|"Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am the worst" (1:15).
B|2TI|Paul, from prison shortly before his death|Around AD 66-67|Paul's last letter: guard the gospel, endure hardship like a soldier, and preach the word.|"Remember Jesus Christ, risen from the dead" (2:8).
B|TIT|Paul|Around AD 63, to Titus in Crete|Appoint good leaders and teach believers to live godly lives because of God's grace.|"The grace of God has appeared… our great God and Savior Jesus Christ" (2:11-13).
B|PHM|Paul, from prison|Around AD 60-62|Paul asks Philemon to welcome back his runaway slave Onesimus as a brother.|Paul offering to pay Onesimus's debt pictures what Jesus does for us.
B|HEB|Unknown|Before AD 70, to Jewish Christians tempted to turn back|Jesus is better: better than angels, Moses, the priests and the sacrifices. So hold on and don't drift.|Jesus is the great high priest and the once-for-all sacrifice.
B|JAS|James, Jesus' brother|Around AD 45-50, one of the earliest New Testament books|Real faith shows up in real life: trials, speech, favoritism, money and humility.|James echoes Jesus' Sermon on the Mount throughout.
B|1PE|Peter|Around AD 62-64, to suffering Christians|You have a living hope; live as holy strangers in this world and endure suffering like Jesus did.|"He himself bore our sins in his body on the tree" (2:24).
B|2PE|Peter, shortly before his death|Around AD 65-67|Grow in godliness, reject false teachers, and remember Jesus will return.|Peter was an eyewitness of Jesus' majesty (1:16).
B|1JN|John|Around AD 85-95|How to know you have eternal life: believe in Jesus, obey his commands and love one another. "God is love."|"The blood of Jesus his Son cleanses us from all sin" (1:7).
B|2JN|John|Around AD 85-95|Walk in truth and love, and don't welcome false teachers.|Confessing that Jesus came in the flesh is the test of truth.
B|3JN|John|Around AD 85-95|Praise for Gaius's hospitality and a warning about Diotrephes.|Faithful believers support those who go out "for the sake of the Name."
B|JUD|Jude, Jesus' brother|Around AD 65-80|Contend for the faith against false teachers, and keep yourselves in God's love.|"To him who is able to keep you from stumbling" (24).
B|REV|John, exiled on Patmos|Around AD 95|A vision of the risen Jesus, the battle behind history, God's judgment on evil, and the new heaven and new earth.|Jesus is the Lamb who was slain, the King of kings, who says "I am making all things new."
