B|HOS|Hosea|Northern Israel, about 750-720 BC|God tells Hosea to marry an unfaithful wife and keep loving her, a living picture of God's relentless love for unfaithful Israel.|Jesus is the faithful husband who pays to bring his unfaithful bride home.
B|JOL|Joel|Judah, date uncertain|A locust plague is a warning of the coming Day of the LORD; return to God, and he will pour out his Spirit on all people.|Peter quotes Joel at Pentecost (Acts 2) when Jesus pours out the Spirit.
B|AMO|Amos, a shepherd from Tekoa|Northern Israel, about 760 BC, a time of wealth|God cares about justice: worship means nothing while the poor are crushed. "Let justice roll down like waters."|Amos ends with God rebuilding David's fallen tent, which Acts 15 applies to Jesus gathering the nations.
B|OBA|Obadiah|After an attack on Jerusalem, likely 586 BC|Edom gloated over Judah's fall, and God will judge pride; the kingdom will be the LORD's.|Jesus brings in the kingdom that belongs to the LORD.
B|JON|Unknown; about Jonah son of Amittai|About 780-750 BC|A prophet runs from God, is swallowed by a fish, and sulks when God shows mercy to Israel's enemies.|Jesus points to "the sign of Jonah": three days and nights, then resurrection (Matthew 12:40).
B|MIC|Micah|Judah, about 735-700 BC|Judgment on corruption, and hope of a ruler from Bethlehem. "Do justice, love mercy, walk humbly with your God."|Micah 5:2 names Bethlehem as the birthplace of the coming ruler: Jesus.
B|NAM|Nahum|About 650-612 BC|God will judge Nineveh, the cruel capital of Assyria.|God's justice against evil is fully dealt with at the cross and Jesus' return.
B|HAB|Habakkuk|Judah, about 605 BC|A prophet asks God hard questions about evil and learns to trust: "The righteous shall live by faith."|Paul builds the gospel on Habakkuk 2:4 (Romans 1:17).
B|ZEP|Zephaniah|Judah under Josiah, about 630 BC|The Day of the LORD is near, but God will save a humble remnant and rejoice over them with singing.|Jesus is the King in the midst of his people who removes judgment.
B|HAG|Haggai|Jerusalem, 520 BC|The returned exiles have neglected God's house; "consider your ways" and rebuild.|God promises to fill the house with glory and shake the nations, fulfilled when Jesus comes to the temple.
B|ZEC|Zechariah|Jerusalem, 520-480 BC|Visions and promises to encourage the builders: God will return, and a humble King will come.|Jesus rides into Jerusalem on a donkey (9:9) and is the pierced one (12:10) and the struck shepherd (13:7).
B|MAL|Malachi|Jerusalem, about 450-430 BC|God confronts half-hearted worship, broken marriages and robbing God, and promises a messenger to prepare the way.|John the Baptist is the messenger, and Jesus is the Lord who suddenly comes to his temple (3:1).
