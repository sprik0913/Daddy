B|MAT|Matthew (Levi), a former tax collector and one of the twelve apostles|Written around AD 60s for Jewish readers|Jesus is the promised Messiah, the King of the Jews, who fulfills the Old Testament and teaches what life in God's kingdom looks like.|Every chapter. Matthew keeps saying "this was to fulfill what was spoken by the prophet."
B|MRK|John Mark, based on Peter's preaching|Rome, around AD 55-65|Fast-moving: Jesus is the Son of God who came "not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many."|The whole book drives toward the cross and the empty tomb.
B|LUK|Luke, a doctor and Paul's companion|Around AD 60, carefully researched for Theophilus|An orderly account of Jesus, the Savior of the world, who seeks and saves the lost, outsiders, women, the poor and sinners.|Luke shows Jesus fulfilling "everything written about me in the Law, the Prophets and the Psalms" (24:44).
B|JHN|John, the "disciple whom Jesus loved"|Around AD 85-95|Written "so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name."|Jesus' "I am" sayings: bread of life, light of the world, good shepherd, the resurrection and the life, the way, truth and life.
B|ACT|Luke|Covers about AD 30-62|The risen Jesus sends his Spirit, and the gospel spreads from Jerusalem to Judea, Samaria and the ends of the earth through Peter, Paul and ordinary believers.|Jesus keeps working from heaven through his Spirit and his church.
MAT 1|Jesus' family line from Abraham through David, and his birth to the virgin Mary: "God with us."
MAT 2|The wise men worship Jesus, Herod kills the boys of Bethlehem, and Joseph flees to Egypt.
MAT 3|John the Baptist prepares the way, and Jesus is baptized; "This is my beloved Son."
MAT 4|Jesus defeats Satan's temptations with Scripture, begins preaching and calls fishermen to follow him.
MAT 5|The Sermon on the Mount begins: the Beatitudes, salt and light, anger, lust, and "love your enemies."
MAT 6|Give, pray (the Lord's Prayer) and fast in secret; don't worry; "seek first the kingdom of God."
MAT 7|Don't judge; ask, seek, knock; the narrow gate; build your house on the rock.
MAT 8|Jesus heals a leper, a centurion's servant and many others, calms a storm and drives out demons.
MAT 9|Jesus forgives and heals a paralyzed man, calls Matthew, and raises a girl; "the harvest is plentiful."
MAT 10|Jesus sends out the twelve and warns of persecution: "Don't fear those who kill the body."
MAT 11|Jesus praises John and invites, "Come to me, all who are weary… and I will give you rest."
MAT 12|Lord of the Sabbath; Pharisees accuse him of working by Satan; the sign of Jonah.
MAT 13|Parables of the kingdom: the sower, weeds, mustard seed, yeast, hidden treasure and the pearl.
MAT 14|John the Baptist is beheaded, Jesus feeds 5,000 and walks on water; Peter sinks.
MAT 15|Real defilement comes from the heart; Jesus heals a Canaanite woman's daughter and feeds 4,000.
MAT 16|Peter confesses, "You are the Christ"; Jesus predicts his death: "Take up your cross and follow me."
MAT 17|The transfiguration; Jesus heals a boy; faith like a mustard seed.
MAT 18|Become like children; leave the 99 for the lost sheep; forgive seventy-seven times.
MAT 19|Marriage and divorce; the rich young ruler walks away; the first will be last.
MAT 20|Workers in the vineyard; "the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve."
MAT 21|Jesus enters Jerusalem on a donkey, clears the temple and tells parables against the leaders.
MAT 22|Parable of the wedding feast; give to Caesar what is Caesar's; the greatest commandment.
MAT 23|Seven woes against hypocritical religious leaders; Jesus weeps over Jerusalem.
MAT 24|Signs of the end and the temple's destruction; be ready, for no one knows the hour.
MAT 25|Parables of the ten virgins and the talents; the sheep and goats: "you did it to me."
MAT 26|Anointed at Bethany, the Last Supper, Gethsemane, betrayal and arrest; Peter denies Jesus.
MAT 27|Jesus is handed to Pilate, crucified and buried; Judas hangs himself.
MAT 28|The empty tomb: "He is risen." The Great Commission: go and make disciples of all nations.
