Why Forged52

A year in the Word, one day at a time.

Forged52 is a simple challenge for men: read the whole Bible in 52 weeks. It isn't about speed or getting it perfect. It's about showing up every morning and letting Scripture shape the man your family follows.

01

Your family follows your lead

Every home has someone out front. Your wife and kids take their cues from how you handle pressure, how you talk, and what you build your life on. Leading them well starts with being led yourself.

02

Steady beats intense

A chapter or two a day, for a year. Some mornings it will hit hard. Some mornings it will feel like nothing happened. Show up anyway. That's how it gets into you.

03

Read it like it matters

If you can, use a paper Bible and leave the phone in another room. Underline what stops you. Write down one line of what God put on your heart. If reading is hard for you, listen instead. The point is the Word, not the format.

04

It outlasts you

Your kids won't remember every lesson you teach them. They will remember what you did every morning. One man's faithfulness can change the direction of a family for generations.

Take the challenge

Three quick steps. No account needed. Your progress is saved on this device.

01Pick your start date

Sundays work well. The mixed plan gives each weekday its own part of the Bible, starting with the letters on Sunday.

02Choose how you'll read

Mixed rotates through the Letters, Law, History, Psalms, Wisdom, Prophets, and Gospels and Acts each week. In order reads the story as it happened, Genesis to Revelation.

03Set a daily reminder

Adds all 364 readings to your phone's calendar, each with the passage in the title.

Bring someone with you

It's easier with a brother next to you. Send him the link and read the same passage each day.
