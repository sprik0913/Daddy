The mission

Every family is a convoy moving through hostile territory.

And every convoy needs a point man. That's you. Out front. Eyes open. Leading your wife, your kids, your household through a culture that wants to pick them off one by one.

You don't get to coast. You don't get to check out. The enemy is real, and he's not taking days off.

Nobody else is coming.

Field manual

Wear that Bible out.

Get a real Bible

Leather, paper, ink. Something you can hold, that will still be on your shelf when your grandkids pull it down.

Put the phone away

No notifications. No "just checking." Fifteen minutes of undivided attention, every single day.

Mark it up

Dog-ear pages. Underline what hits you in the chest. Argue with it in the margins.

Write it down

Write what God puts on your heart. One line is enough. A year from now you'll read it back and see the man you were becoming.

Let it burn
