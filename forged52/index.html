↻Already reading? Pick up where you are

Coming from the Forged52 app or another plan? Enter the week and day you're on today. Your dates line up and everything before it is marked complete.

01Pick your start date

Sundays work best. The plan runs Sunday to Saturday each week, just like the Forged52 app.

02Choose how you'll read

Topical: each day of the week covers a different part of the Bible: Letters (Sun), Foundations (Mon), The Story (Tue), Prayer & Worship (Wed), Wisdom (Thu), The Prophets (Fri) and Jesus (Sat). Chronological: the Bible in the order events happened, from Creation to Revelation.
